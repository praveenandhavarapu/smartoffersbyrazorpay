How it works

Three steps, every week

01 / FIND
Spot wasted offers

Reads your coupons and orders, and finds discounts that went to shoppers who'd buy anyway.

02 / DECIDE
Pick the cheapest offer that works

Starts from 180 similar stores, then switches to your own results. No offer when none pays back.

03 / PROVE
Measure against a holdout

1 in 10 shoppers sees no offer. The difference is the real lift, and it's what you pay on.

What the agent can and can't do

You stay in control

Read checkout, order and coupon data
Run approved offers on Magic Checkout, within budget
Simulate plans before you approve
Pause an offer that drops below your break-even
Change product prices
Go over your budget or launch offers without approval
Works alongside

Recovery agents bring shoppers back. Smart Offers decides what to offer them.
